      Notes on the views and the sensor board
      
Side view. The grey strip past the arm end is the sensor array along the arm's axis: ultrasonic at the arm end, sound sensor 42 mm out (blue), colour sensor 83 mm out at the tip (red). Their 45 mm sideways offsets do not show here. The red bar is the 30 mm button pusher under the arm end. The dashed line is the gap from whichever sensor is aimed at the current target.

      
Top view. To scale for the current pose. The sideways thickness of each arm seen from above is estimated from the photos (main arm 30 mm, second arm 20 mm); the 50 mm and 40 mm side faces show in the side view.

      
Plan view. 400 mm half circle from the brief. Black dot: ultrasonic. Red: colour sensor, 45 mm to your right facing the machine. Blue: sound sensor, 45 mm to your left.

1. Platform and base
Side view. The base hub sits on the 20 mm platform with the base servo (D2) inside it; the arched bracket on top holds the two main arm servos. Everything above the hub's ring turns with the base servo. Hub size (about 120 mm across, 60 mm tall) is estimated from the photos and only used for drawing.

    
2. Main arm
Side face 50 mm wide, 225 mm pivot to pivot. Driven at the bottom by two servos facing each other, left D3 and right D4, which always turn in opposite directions. 0 = upright, +50 forward, -15 back.

    
3. Second arm
Open frame, side face 40 mm wide, 160 mm from its pivot to the end. Servo D5 at the pivot. 0 = level, +45 up, -90 down.

    
4. Sensor array (from above)
Runs along the axis of the second arm from its end. Ultrasonic on the crossbar at the centre, sound sensor on your left leg, colour sensor at the tip of your right leg (you standing in front of the machine).

    
5. Button pusher
70 mm bar fixed under the sensor array at the end of the second arm, square to the arm. At Point E the tip moves in a straight line along the button's own axis, from 44 mm off it to 8 mm in, because the button sits on the stand's sloping top.

    
6. Assembled at zero
Base forward, main arm upright, second arm level. Heights from the table.

  


  
  

    

      
Breadboard layout

      

        
      

      
Servo power comes from an external 5 V supply on the top rail; the sensors run from the Uno 5 V on the bottom rail. Both grounds are joined. Hover or tap a part in the table to highlight its wires.
