MECN3066A Group 21 / Robotic arm

Arm bench

Runs the same joint maths and task sequence as arm_controller.ino, with the arm drawn to scale. Drive the joints by hand, send it to a station, or play the whole demonstration and read the serial output it would print.

Side view

Drawn in the vertical plane the base is facing. Millimetres from the base axis and from the base plate. The grey plate at the arm end is the sensor board: ultrasonic at its centre, sound sensor 42 mm down (blue), colour sensor 83 mm down (red). Their 45 mm sideways offsets do not show here. The red bar is the 50 mm button pusher, fixed 20 mm out and pointing straight down when the second arm is level. The dashed line is the standoff from whichever sensor is aimed at the current target.

Plan view

400 mm half circle from the brief. The bold line is where the arm faces. Black dot: ultrasonic. Red: colour sensor, 45 mm to your right facing the machine. Blue: sound sensor, 45 mm to your left.

Servo power comes from an external 5 V supply on the top rail; the sensors run from the Uno 5 V on the bottom rail. Both grounds are joined. Hover or tap a part in the table to highlight its wires.
